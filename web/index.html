Z crash guard

The spare Z switch cuts all motion if it's pressed while the printer is homing or probing. It's set to click when the nozzle is ~0.8 mm above the bed.

Bed level map

Numbers are mm above (+) or below (−) the centre, seen from above with the back of the bed at the top. The printer corrects for this automatically.
